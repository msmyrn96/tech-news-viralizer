# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tech professionals, developers, and power news readers who want high-signal information fast. They open Viralizer during a break or at the start of the day to scan what actually mattered in tech — not everything, just the stories that broke through. They're impatient with noise.

## Product Purpose

An AI-ranked global tech news aggregator that scores articles by virality and surfaces only the stories worth reading. Success looks like a user scanning the feed and immediately knowing what the internet is talking about — without reading a word of filler.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, Signal, Ruthless. No decoration for decoration's sake. Every element either carries information or gets cut. The interface should feel like a tool built by someone who reads too much tech news and got tired of bad signal-to-noise.

## Anti-references

- Generic news sites (CNN, BBC web): cluttered, ad-heavy, low density of real signal
- Anything that pads content to feel "complete" rather than useful

## Design Principles

1. **Signal over surface.** If an element doesn't carry information, remove it. Every pixel is accountable.
2. **Density is a feature.** Compact, scannable, no wasted vertical space. Users are here to scan, not linger.
3. **The ranking is the product.** Virality score is the reason this exists. It should never be hidden or de-emphasized.
4. **Dark because it's a tool, not an aesthetic.** Late-night reading, terminal-adjacent, eye-safe for extended use.
5. **No false urgency.** No red badges, no pulsing "breaking news" patterns. The score does the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Keyboard navigable. Reduced motion respected.
